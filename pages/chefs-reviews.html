Kitchen & Espresso Brigade

Meet Our Culinary Team

Every plate and pour at SavorSpot pairs classical heirloom technique with modern precision over live charcoal and single-origin roast profiles.

ZK
Executive Chef

Chef Zayd Kamal

16 Years Experience • Islamabad & Istanbul

Specialty

24-Hour Marrow Braises & Kashmiri Saffron Reductions

Trained in classical Anatolian fire-cooking and Lahori heirloom kitchens, Chef Zayd leads SavorSpot's tasting menu — balancing slow-rendered spices with crisp, modern plating on matte obsidian stoneware.

MN
Head Pastry & Saffronier

Chef Meher Nisar

11 Years Experience • Lahore & Paris

Specialty

Laminated Espresso Viennoiserie & Spun Amber Tarts

Chef Meher bridges French patisserie structure with northern Pakistani botanicals, crafting delicate mille-feuille layers infused with dark roast espresso, roasted pistachio frangipane, and cardamom blossom.

TH
Master Grill & Smoke Artisan

Chef Tariq Hayat

14 Years Experience • Peshawar & Dubai

Specialty

Binchotan Charcoal Lamb & Wild Mountain Herb Crusts

Working exclusively over aged olive-wood and binchotan embers, Chef Tariq oversees our dry-aging cellar and live grill station, locking deep smoke aroma into every prime cut without masking delicate marbling.

AR
Head Barista & Elixir Curator

Amina Raza

9 Years Experience • Islamabad & Melbourne

Specialty

Smoked Botanical Elixirs & Single-Origin Espresso

Amina curates SavorSpot's zero-proof pairing program and small-batch espresso bar, blending Ethiopian micro-lots with cardamom mist, rosewater reductions, and torched cinnamon aromatics.
